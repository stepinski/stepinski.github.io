<div class="about-bio">
  <p class="role">CTO & Co-founder, Infinitii AI · Gdańsk, Poland</p>

  <p>I build ML systems for municipal water utilities. Specifically: physics-informed time-series models that predict sewer overflow events before they happen, giving operations teams hours of lead time instead of seconds of alarm.</p>

  <p>Our core production system is <a href="https://github.com/carldata">Lark</a>, a Go library validated on Peel Region, Ontario — 13 storms, zero false alarms, with one real overflow caught 7 hours and 40 minutes before it occurred.</p>

  <p>I've been building sensor and telemetry platforms for water infrastructure for 11 years. Before that: C++ (IEC 61850, native desktop GIS), Scala ML infrastructure, Python data pipelines. My current technical focus is Go, and has been for a few years now.</p>
</div>

## Work

At [Infinitii AI](https://infinitii.ai), I lead the engineering team building a physics-informed ML platform for water utility clients. We work with municipal operators across North America, helping them make sense of telemetry that's noisy, sparse, and attached to infrastructure that can't fail.

## Writing

I write about physics-informed ML, water infrastructure, Go, local-first software, and the occasional reflection on building technical teams. The through-line is usually: *how do you build things that last?*
